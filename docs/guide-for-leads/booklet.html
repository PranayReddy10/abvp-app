Introduction

One app, everything a delegate needs

Thousands of karyakartas will reach Bhagyanagar from every prant. Most have never been to the city. ABVP Connect puts their stay, transport, food, help desks and emergency contacts in their pocket – with a real person to call for each one.

Why this matters

  • No more calling around. A delegate landing at the airport at midnight sees exactly who to call at that desk.
  • Every lead is one tap away. Each place in the guide shows its lead and team with Call and WhatsApp buttons.
  • Open to everyone. Guests do not need to register or sign in to see the guide. We do not need a list of guests.
  • In their language. English, हिन्दी and తెలుగు, switched with one tap.
  • Help that reaches the right team. Delegates can chat with the help desk or raise a ticket; the team replies from the office.

Two parts

The app – what delegates and members use on their phones.
The admin panel – a website where our office team fills in the guide and answers help requests.

Getting started

Opening the app

Anyone can open the conference guide straight away. Signing in is only needed to chat with the help desk and to use member features.

Guide card on the first screen
Choose a language
The same screen in Telugu

What delegates see

  • The guide card sits at the top of the first screen. Tap it – no account needed.
  • Near the event (from two days before), the guide opens by itself when the app starts. "Skip" goes to the normal app.
  • Signing in uses the mobile number and a 6-digit SMS code – no passwords. "Continue with Google" also works.

Languages

  • Tap the translate button (top right) and choose English, हिन्दी or తెలుగు. Also under Me → Language.
  • The app remembers the choice. The first time, it follows the phone's language.
  • Names and details typed by organisers appear as written – write important ones in two languages.

Emergency · आपातकाल

In an emergency nobody should have to search. One big button calls the ambulance, and every hall has its own emergency leads.

Big call button and numbers
Emergency leads by hall

What delegates see

  • A large pulsing 108 button – one tap calls the ambulance.
  • A row of numbers: 108 ambulance, 112 police / any emergency, 181 women helpline.
  • Emergency leads by hall: Medical room, Hall A, Food court, Halls C and D, Exhibition, Nivas blocks, Mahila Sahayata – each with its lead and team to call.
  • A note for each hall, e.g. "Doctor on duty day and night; ambulance waits at Gate 4".

Emergency leads

  • Give one lead and a deputy for each hall.
  • Phones on and charged at all times.
  • Know where the medical room and ambulance are.

Support · सहायता

For everything that is not an emergency: a question, a problem with the room, something lost. Delegates can chat or raise a ticket.

Chat or raise a ticket
A reply from the help desk

How it works

  • Chat with the help desk – type a question; the reply appears in a few seconds, like a messaging app.
  • Raise a ticket – choose stay, food, transport, lost and found, medical… and say where you are.
  • "My conversations" shows every chat with its status: New, Being handled, Resolved.
  • Delegates get a notification when the team replies.
  • Without signing in, Support shows the helpline number instead.

Help desk team

  • Answer from the admin panel's Help desk – it updates by itself.
  • Mark a conversation Resolved when done.

Help

Questions people ask

Does a guest need to register or sign in?

No. The conference guide opens for anyone who installs the app. Signing in is only needed to chat with the help desk.

Do we need a list of guests?

No. The guide is open to all. Our own team members are assigned duties separately.

Which languages?

English, हिन्दी and తెలుగు. Choose with the translate button. More languages can be added later.

Does it need internet?

Yes, to load the guide and to chat. The event pass works without internet once it has been opened.

What if someone has no smartphone?

Arrival desks and offices still help in person. The helpline works from any phone.

How fast does the help desk reply?

Messages reach the help desk at once. Replies appear on the delegate's phone within a few seconds.

Who can change the guide?

Organisers with access to the admin panel. Changes show in the app immediately.

Is my phone number public?

Numbers you give for the guide can be seen by anyone who opens it. You may give a desk number instead.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. The first time, you also confirm your mobile number once; after that Google signs you in.

Something is wrong in the app?

Tell the organising office or raise a ticket under Support → Raise a ticket.

In one line

Every delegate should always know where to go and whom to call. With your help, ABVP Connect makes that true for the Rashtriya Adhiveshan.
