ABVP Connect

The everyday home of every karyakarta: their profile and dayitva, their Vibhags, their next baithak, and the circulars meant for them. On event day, it tells them in five seconds where to go, what to do, and whom to report to.

My thoughts on the plan

What I would keep, add and cut

The two documents are thorough and the flow is right. These are the changes I would make so the product feels simple to a first-year student and stays affordable on Hostinger.

Keep

One app for everyone, role-aware

Members, guests, coordinators and check-in volunteers all use the same Flutter app. Extra tools (Scan, Team) appear only when that person holds a role for an event, and disappear after it closes. No separate "volunteer app" to install.

Add

Event-day mode

On the day of a registered event, Home turns into a single saffron card: today's duty, reporting point, gate, team lead's call button, and the pass one tap away. Everything else steps back.

Add

Bilingual from day one

English plus Hindi at launch, with Telugu and other state languages added as plain translation files. Switching language is on the sign-in screen, not hidden in settings. Fonts chosen here render Devanagari and Latin from one family.

Add

Pass that works with no signal

Large venues lose network. The QR pass is cached on the phone as a signed, time-limited token, and scanners queue check-ins offline and sync when back. Members never see a spinner at the gate.

Add

Scope switcher in the admin

A person can be District Admin of Hyderabad and Event Coordinator of one conference. The admin top bar always shows the active scope ("Telangana › Hyderabad") and every number on screen follows it. This prevents most "why can I see this?" confusion.

Add

Daily use before event use

Big events happen a few times a year; baithaks, circulars and Vibhag work happen every week. If the app is only for events, people uninstall it between them. Profiles, Vibhags, meetings and circulars ship first so the app is already on every phone when the first event opens.

Add

Meetings by link, not by video hosting

Anyone with the right role schedules a baithak and pastes a Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Jitsi or YouTube Live link. The app handles invites, RSVP, reminders, attendance and minutes. Video itself stays with Google or Zoom, which keeps Hostinger cost near zero.

Defer

Heavy logistics later

Food, accommodation, transport and live location are a second product. Pilot the everyday features and the event slice in one state first, then add logistics module by module.

Feature map

The whole organisation in one app

Everything a karyakarta, an office bearer or a Vibhag coordinator does through the year, grouped by what people are trying to get done. The tag shows which phase I would build it in.

People and profiles

Rich profile P1Photo, bio, institution, course and year, languages, skills (design, speaking, writing, first aid), social links.
Dayitva history P1Every responsibility held, with level, place and years: Ikai Mantri 2023, Zilla Sanyojak 2025. Added by the admin who assigns it, so it can be trusted.
Directory P2Find office bearers by role, place or Vibhag. Phone numbers stay hidden; the member taps "Request contact" or "Schedule a call".
Privacy controls P1Each person chooses who sees phone, email, college and birthday: nobody, my unit, my Vibhags, or office bearers.

Organisation

Vibhag spaces P2Each of the ~70 Vibhags gets its own space: coordinators, members, updates, meetings, events and resources. Members join one or many.
Unit and college pages P2Every ikai and college has its committee, member count, upcoming work and recent activity.
Circulars with read receipts P2National, kshetra, state or district sends a circular to a chosen audience. The sender sees who has read it and can remind the rest.
Sadasyata drive P3Each member gets a personal join link and QR. Units see targets and progress during the membership campaign.

Meetings and calls

Schedule a baithak P2Online with any link, in person with a venue, or both. Invite a Vibhag, a unit, a role (all Zilla Sanyojaks in Telangana) or individuals.
One-to-one calls P2From a profile, "Schedule a call" proposes a time and a link. The other person accepts or suggests another time.
RSVP, reminders, attendance P2Going / Maybe / Can't. Reminder a day before and 15 minutes before. Tapping "Join" marks attendance. Add to Google Calendar.
Minutes and action items P3The organiser records karyavrutt and decisions; action items get an owner and a due date and show on that person's Home.

Field work

Activity reports P2After any programme, the unit reports type, date, attendance and photos in under a minute. This feeds every dashboard upward.
Campaigns and andolan P3A campaign page with goal, dates, materials and a live count of programmes reported against it.
Polls and surveys P3Quick polls inside a Vibhag or unit; structured surveys for campus issues, with results only for the organiser.

Events

Events, registration, pass P3Everything in the first design: eligibility, waitlist, offline pass, gate scanning, guests.
Duties and command centre P4Teams, duties with reporting person, live arrivals and exceptions.
Logistics P5Stay, food, transport, consent-based live location.

Learning and resources

Resource library P2Pamphlets, posters, geet with audio, speeches, training notes. Filtered by Vibhag and language, downloadable for offline use.
Abhyas varg tracking P3Training camps attended, shown on the profile, with a certificate the member can download.
Help and feedback P2Ask the right office for help, report a profile error, or send a suggestion.

Design system

Saffron for action, ink for everything else

Saffron is used only where the person must act or look: the primary button, the pass tab, today's card. The rest is quiet ink and grey, so the screen stays calm even with a lot of data. Status always shows as a coloured pill with words, never colour alone.

Rules

  • One primary (saffron) button per screen.
  • Touch targets at least 44 px; body text never below 14 px on phones.
  • Phone numbers are never shown in lists; a call button appears only where the role allows it.
  • Light and dark themes from the same tokens.

Mobile app · Flutter · Android and iOS

Joining and event screens

Bottom navigation has five places: Home, Calendar, Pass, Vibhags, Me. Calendar holds events, meetings and duties together. Pass sits in the middle and raised, because at the gate it is the only thing that matters. Scroll sideways to see all screens.

App navigation

HomeToday card, next meeting, unread circulars, action items, member ID
CalendarEvents, meetings, calls and duties in one list; register, RSVP, acknowledge
PassEvent QR (or member QR when no event is active)
VibhagsMy Vibhags and unit, updates, resources, directory, join more
MeProfile, dayitva history, privacy, documents, activity reports, language, help

Role tools inside the same app

ScanCheck-in volunteers: scan pass, see name and photo, mark arrived. Works offline, syncs later.
TeamTeam leads: see their team, mark duties in progress, done or blocked.
GuestGuests: invitation, pass, venue and contact only. No member features.
ApproveDistrict admins on the move: a simple approvals list. Full review stays in the web admin.

Mobile app · everyday organisation

Profiles, Vibhags and meetings

The screens people will open every week. Scheduling a call or baithak takes one form: paste any meeting link, choose who is invited, and the app handles invites, reminders and attendance.

Web admin · React, built as static files

An admin panel that only shows your scope

A fixed left sidebar, a scope switcher at the top, and content below. The panel is designed for a district office laptop at 1366 px. Switch between three key screens.

Running on Hostinger shared hosting

Designed to fit the hosting you already have

Shared hosting runs PHP well and nothing long-running. So the API is Laravel, the admin is a static React build, background work runs from cron in small batches, and live screens refresh by polling. Nothing in the design depends on a server that stays awake.

People use
Flutter appAndroid and iOS. Caches pass, duties and event details for offline use.
Admin paneladmin.abvpconnect.inStatic React files, no Node server needed.
Public websiteabvpconnect.inDownload links, event pages, guest invitation links.
Hostinger shared plan
Laravel APIapi.abvpconnect.in → /public onlyREST /api/v1, roles and scopes checked on every request, audit log.
MySQL (Hostinger, same server)My suggestion for the pilot: no outbound-connection risk and nothing extra to pay. Move to managed PostgreSQL or MySQL when you scale.
Cron, every minuteschedule:run · queue:work --stop-when-emptySends push and SMS in batches, builds report exports, expires passes.
Outside services
DigitalOcean SpacesPhotos and documents. Private files through short-lived signed links.
SMS OTP providerDLT-registered Indian sender, for example MSG91.
Firebase Cloud MessagingPush notifications, free tier.
MapsOpen in Google Maps by link. No paid map SDK in phase 1.
Shared hosting limitHow the design responds
No WebSockets or always-on processesCommand centre polls every 15–20 s and shows "Updated 12 s ago" so nobody expects instant updates.
CPU and memory caps, traffic spikesPass check-ins go through one small endpoint; scanners batch offline check-ins. Event lists are cached as JSON for 60 s.
Limited disk and slow uploadsPhotos are resized on the phone to under 300 KB before upload, and stored on Spaces, not Hostinger.
No video or call hostingMeetings store only a link; Google Meet, Zoom or Jitsi carry the video. The API makes a small calendar file for "Add to Google Calendar". Reminders go out from the same cron job.
Cron runs at most every minuteNotifications say "within a few minutes"; OTP SMS is sent directly in the request, not queued.
When to move offBefore the first multi-state event with more than about 5,000 people on one day, move the API to a Hostinger VPS. The code does not change.
