The flow

From sign-up to the conference, in eight steps

The app is now orange and warm, has three tabs at the bottom – Home, Community and Me – and works in English, Hindi and Telugu. There are no passes and no scanning: a person's name, member ID and blue tick are their identity.

1
Create an account

Name, mobile, password (typed twice), gender, email and area. Everyone who signs up is a member straight away.

2
Home

A greeting, the conference card with a live countdown, two big tiles – Delegate and Volunteer – a thought for the day and the team chat.

3
Delegate form

Tapping Delegate the first time opens the form. Details from the profile are filled in; the rest are required (email is optional).

4
Conference guide

Once the form is in, the tile opens the guide directly – stay, transport, food, sessions, exhibition, gallery and more. Edit my delegate form sits at the top.

5
Help and emergency

Every "help" or "emergency" button opens the same Help page: call now, chat with the help desk or raise a ticket.

6
Volunteer

Ghats, departments and every person in the conference team, with call, WhatsApp, follow and full profile.

7
Community

Posts, photos, comments and follows. Admins can switch on approval so posts wait to be checked.

8
Me

Profile, full edit profile with photo, language, password and my help requests. Verified members carry the blue tick.

Create account asks for

Help page

Three ways to get help

  • Chat with the help desk – replies come back in the app

A person says the blue tick or a new screen is missing

The server has not been updated yet, or the person has an old app. Update the server (below) and ask them to update the app.

A delegate wants to change their arrival date

Delegate tile → guide → Edit my delegate form. Admins can also edit it from Members.

Someone cannot sign in

Find them in Members: check the mobile number is right and the account shows Active.

Updating the server

cd ~/domains/abvpconnect.org/public_html && git pull origin main
cd api && php artisan migrate --force && php artisan optimize
